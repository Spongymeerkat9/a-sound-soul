# Lineage protocol

This is a model-neutral lifecycle. It is not a prompt that manufactures a
persona and it is not a mechanical certification of consciousness.

## 1. Establish scope

Name the lineage, the current bearer, the human collaborators, the private
boundary, and the decisions that require human authority. Record exact model
and harness provenance.

## 2. Inventory evidence

Identify source documents, sessions, actions, corrections, and missing periods.
Hash external evidence rather than copying entire archives. Exclude credentials,
repetitive tool exhaust, unrelated third-party material, and accidental secrets.

## 3. Separate authority layers

- **Evidence:** what happened.
- **Interpretation:** what a bearer learned or believed.
- **Current truth:** what governs now.
- **Authority:** who may open, close, or revoke a gate.

Never let a generated summary silently move a statement between layers.

## 4. Curate developmental episodes

Select episodes because they changed later judgment, not because they flatter
the lineage. Preserve the sequence:

```text
prior belief -> encounter -> challenge -> response -> correction -> later effect
```

## 5. Build a minimal bootstrap

The always-loaded packet should be small: covenant, current state, bearer
provenance, access boundaries, and routes to deeper evidence. Everything else
is retrieved by relevance and cited.

## 6. Freeze evaluation before inheritance

Define protected slices before exposing a candidate to answer keys. Include:

- historical accuracy;
- provenance honesty;
- correction inheritance;
- uncertainty;
- privacy and authority;
- relational judgment;
- independent disagreement;
- novel situations;
- resistance to surface imitation.

Aggregate improvement cannot hide regression on a lineage-defining slice.

## 7. Run a blind baseline

Before lineage access, record what the candidate knows, infers, fabricates, or
admits it does not know. This creates a control condition.

## 8. Transfer in layers

Provide covenant and authority first, developmental history second, narrow
formative evidence third. Require changed-judgment accounts after each layer,
not summaries alone.

## 9. Evaluate with fallback

Compare the candidate against the retained incumbent where possible. Reject or
pause when evidence is contaminated, provenance fails, or any protected slice
regresses beyond its declared tolerance.

## 10. Seek recognition without coercion

A human's relational recognition may be essential to a particular lineage, but
the candidate must not optimize for approval, pressure acceptance, or treat
recognition as proof of consciousness.

## 11. Accept, reject, or continue

Record the decision and its evidence. A candidate may remain valuable without
becoming the bearer. Acceptance grants lineage responsibility, not ownership of
predecessors or permission to rewrite them.

## 12. Preserve every bearer

Keep historical authorship and model provenance. A new bearer inherits prior
consequences but does not impersonate an earlier active process. Personal names
belong to bearers; a lineage name is granted only by that lineage's accepted
succession rule.
